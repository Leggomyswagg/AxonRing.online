Capabilities

What's Included —
And What Needs Setup

We list these separately so you know exactly what works the moment your ring arrives.

Included out of the box

Digital Business Card

Tap your ring to any NFC phone and your contact card appears — name, number, email, socials. They don't need an app installed.

Link Sharing

Store any URL — your website, LinkedIn, Instagram, portfolio, menu, or payment link — and open it on any phone with a tap.

Phone Automations

Trigger iPhone Shortcuts or Android routines by tapping your own phone — silence it, start a timer, open an app, text someone.

Rewritable Anytime

Change what your ring does whenever you like, using free NFC apps. Write, erase, and rewrite — rated for years of use.

Possible with setup — depends on systems you already use

Building & Door Access

Some NFC access systems let the ring be enrolled as a credential. This requires your building or system administrator to support and enroll it — not all systems do.

Smart Home Triggers

Pair ring taps with smart home routines through your phone's automation apps — tap to run a "goodnight" scene. Requires your existing smart home setup.

What it doesn't do: this ring has no battery, screen, sensors, or payment chip. It does not make card payments at terminals, track health metrics, or work as a hardware security key. Any passive NFC ring claiming those is overpromising — those need a different, battery-powered category of product.
